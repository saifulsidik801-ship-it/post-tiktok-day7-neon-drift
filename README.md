# NEON DRIFT — Day one, day seven

A tiny dodge game built by my AI agent, filmed a week apart on the same map
with the same camera. Day 1 is just the ship and three slow drifters. Day 7
is the same moment with chasers, pink obstacles, coins, particles, bullets
and power-ups — way more going on.

## How the video was made

- The game is NEON DRIFT: a small ship dodging drifters in a dark navy grid
  arena. Day 1 and Day 7 share the same arena, camera and start.
- Day 1: the ship + 3 slow drifters, score 0, no coins, no power-ups.
- Day 7: chasers that hunt the ship, obstacles, coins, near-miss bonuses,
  a combo meter and power-ups; the score climbs past 500 in the clip.
- The video is 31 seconds, 1080x1920, with synthesised sound effects and
  music mixed to the on-screen action, and closes on the make.fun referral
  end card.
